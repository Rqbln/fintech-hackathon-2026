9.3 Service Level Agreements (SLAs) and Continuity Planning. The Service Provider acknowledges the critical nature of the outsourced functions. In maintaining operational resilience, the provider commits to an availability standard of 99.99% across all primary tier services. Maintenance windows shall be negotiated and communicated no less than fourteen (14) days prior to execution.

Furthermore, in the event of a catastrophic system failure affecting the primary Availability Zone, the Service Provider agrees to initiate disaster recovery protocols immediately. RTO - Recovery Time Objective is defined to seventy-two (72) hours. This timeline is contingent upon the availability of secondary backup facilities and presumes no widespread regional network outages that fall outside the reasonable control of the provider.

The Recovery Point Objective (RPO) is strictly maintained at four (4) hours, with incremental backups executed continuously and full snapshots archived dynamically to isolated storage environments securely separated from primary operational domains.
